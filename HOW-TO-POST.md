# How to post news on Mirror Lilong (from your phone)

1. **Open the admin panel.** In your phone's browser go to **mirror-lilong.netlify.app/admin** (save it to your home screen). Tap **Sign in with GitHub** and log in with your GitHub account. You only need to do this once per phone.

2. **Post a new story.** Tap **News stories**, then **New** (the + button). Fill in:
   - **Headline**, **Section** (Manipur, Northeast, India, World, Sports or Entertainment) and a **Short summary**
   - **Photo**: tap it to upload from your gallery. Big phone photos are shrunk automatically.
   - **Story text**: leave an empty line between paragraphs
   - Optional: your **Facebook post link** and any **Source links**
   - Switches: **Big story at the top**, **Show in the BREAKING ticker**, **Show in Top Stories**, **Trending**, **Video story**

   Then tap **Save**. The story is live on the website in about 1 minute (pull down to refresh the site).

3. **Edit a story.** Tap **News stories**, tap the story, change anything (headline, text, photo, switches), and tap **Save**.

4. **Delete a story.** Open the story, tap the **⋮** (more) menu at the top, and choose **Delete**. To hide a story without deleting it, turn on **Hide this story from the website** and tap **Save**.

5. **Change the big top story or the breaking ticker.** Turn the switch on for the stories you want. The newest story with **Big story at the top** switched on becomes the main story on the home page. If none is switched on, the newest story is used.

Videos page links (Facebook, YouTube, Instagram) are under **Videos page** in the admin panel.
