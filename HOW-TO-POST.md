# How to post news on Mirror Lilong (from your phone)

Live site: https://mirrorlilong.github.io/mirror-lilong/

1. Open **app.pagescms.org** in your phone browser and tap **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. First time only: GitHub asks to install **Pages CMS**. Choose **Only select repositories**, pick **mirror-lilong**, and tap **Install**. Then open **mirror-lilong**. (Tip: add the page to your home screen.)
3. **New story:** tap **News stories**, then **Add an entry**. Fill in Headline, Section, Photo (upload from your gallery) and Story text (leave an empty line between paragraphs). Turn on "Big story at the top" or "BREAKING ticker" if you want, then tap **Save**.
4. **Edit:** open the story, change anything, tap **Save**.
5. **Delete:** open the story, tap the **⋯** menu and choose **Delete**. To hide a story without deleting it, turn on "Hide this story" and save.

Each save goes live on the website in about 1-2 minutes. Refresh the site to see it.
